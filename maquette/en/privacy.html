Privacy policy

How data is handled when you visit fangys.hk.

On this page
  1. Website operator
  2. Visiting the website
  3. Contact enquiries
  4. Use and retention
  5. Your rights

Website operator

FANGYS CORPORATION LIMITED publishes this website. Vercel provides hosting.

Visiting the website

To deliver and secure the site, the hosting provider may process technical data such as IP addresses and request information. Its Privacy Notice describes its data practices.

Contact enquiries

Submitting the form sends your name, email address, organisation if provided, subject and message to the contact function hosted by Vercel. When automatic delivery is enabled, EmailJS forwards the enquiry to info@fangys.hk. If delivery is unavailable, you can send the draft from your own email application.

Use and retention

Contact information is used to handle and respond to your enquiry. The website does not retain drafts. Received messages may be kept for as long as needed to handle the enquiry and meet applicable obligations. Technical data is handled according to the hosting provider’s practices.

Your rights

Depending on applicable law, you may request access to or correction of your personal data by emailing info@fangys.hk. The Hong Kong PCPD describes local procedures.
